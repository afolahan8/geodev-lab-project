# Assessing Paved Road Accessibility in Alimosho Local Government Area, Lagos State

## The Question

Which residential areas in Alimosho Local Government Area, Lagos State, are located more than 500 metres from a paved road, and where are the major accessibility gaps?

## Why It Matters

Road accessibility influences mobility, economic activity, access to healthcare, education, emergency services, and overall quality of life. Although Alimosho is one of the most populous local government areas in Lagos State, road infrastructure is not evenly distributed across all communities. Identifying areas that lack adequate access to paved roads can support infrastructure planning, prioritisation of road improvement projects, and evidence-based decision-making.

## The Data I Need

- Alimosho LGA administrative boundary
- Road network data
- Road surface information identifying paved and unpaved roads
- Settlement or residential area data
- Building footprint data (if available)

## What I Would Build

I will build a spatial accessibility assessment that identifies residential areas in Alimosho LGA with limited access to paved roads. The project will produce maps and statistics showing areas located more than 500 metres from paved roads and highlight major accessibility gaps across the local government area. Over the twelve-month GeoDev Lab programme, this work can be expanded into an interactive web map and decision-support system for analysing road accessibility and infrastructure needs.
