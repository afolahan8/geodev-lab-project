# Assessing Paved Road Accessibility in Alimosho Local Government Area, Lagos State

## The Question

Which residential areas in Alimosho Local Government Area, Lagos State, are located more than 500 metres from a paved road, and where are the major accessibility gaps?

## The Data I Need

- Alimosho LGA administrative boundary
- Road network data
- Road surface information identifying paved and unpaved roads
- Settlement or residential area data
- Building footprint data (if available)

## Where the Data Comes From

| Dataset | Source | Link |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Alimosho administrative boundary | GRID3 Nigeria | https://data.grid3.org |
| Road network | OpenStreetMap | https://www.openstreetmap.org |
| Road surface information | OpenStreetMap (`surface=*`) | https://www.openstreetmap.org |
| Settlement extents | GRID3 Nigeria | https://data.grid3.org |
| Building footprints | OpenStreetMap | https://www.openstreetmap.org |
